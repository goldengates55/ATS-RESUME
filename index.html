Occupation data: NOC 2021 v1.0 (built in)

Built in: ESCO v1.2.1 in English and French (the language follows your job posting or resume), loaded when you choose ESCO, and NOC 2021 Version 1.0 for Canada (official, English). To use a newer NOC version, load your own CSV with columns code,title,teer,duties,examples. To use ESCO in another language or a newer version, load the ESCO CSV package (.zip) from esco.ec.europa.eu; the page remembers it in this browser. Verify every code.

US: O*NET-SOC (O*NET 31.0) is built in. UK: SOC 2020 is built in (unit group descriptions plus the coding index of about 32,000 job titles), loaded when you choose SOC 2020. Australia: OSCA 2024 v1.0 is built in the same way. New Zealand: NOL V3.0 is built in as interim data (codes and titles with the V2 and V3 changes applied, and no official descriptions yet). To replace it, choose NOL and load the full official file from Stats NZ. ANZSCO has been replaced in both countries.

Custom synonyms

Built-in synonyms are always on (for example ML, machine learning; JS, JavaScript; k8s, Kubernetes). Add your own, one group per line: ML = machine learning = deep learning. The first term is the one shown in reports.

Pre-filled numbers

Suggested lines come with example numbers already in place, so they are ready to add. Examples fit the role type found in your resume (you can change it) and how senior the title sounds. Pick how strong the examples should be, and change any number with Change numbers under a line.

Adds the missing posting keywords to Skills, writes a summary if yours is empty, adds every missing official duty with example numbers, then re-scans and shows the new match score. Undo puts everything back.

Tailor to this posting

Builds a plan to point your resume at this posting, all on this device. It puts the posting's keywords first in Skills, moves your most relevant bullets to the top of each role, and writes a summary aimed at the role. Nothing new is claimed unless you tick the add-on items below, and those need the confirmation box above. Review the plan, untick anything you do not want, then apply. Keywords are now weighted by how often and how firmly the posting asks for them (and matched through synonyms), and wording the posting already uses for the same thing (for example ML and machine learning) can be swapped in. You get a line-by-line view of what changed, and the result can be saved as a named version. Undo puts everything back.

Fit to a page limit

Measures how many pages your resume runs in the builder's PDF layout. If it runs over, it first tightens spacing and margins a little (text never goes below 9.5 pt), then removes the least relevant bullets (using the posting if there is one, and always keeping at least 2 per role) until it fits. You see every removed line, and Undo puts them back.

TEER upgrade (Canada, NOC)

Finds your latest role's NOC match and TEER level, then shows the closest occupations one or more TEER levels up in the same field (TEER 0 is the top level, 5 the lowest), with their typical entry requirements. For each one it lists official duties your resume does not yet show. Tick only the duties you have truly performed, tick “I confirm” above, and add them to your latest role. A higher TEER is only honest if the work was real: leave out anything you have not done.

Push my TEER match to 100%

For every role in the builder, finds the occupation it matches and the fewest official duty lines that lift that match to 100% (the top score, 1.00). You see each role's score before and after, can untick any line, and nothing is added until you tick “I confirm” above and press Apply. These lines are the official wording for the role, so keep only the ones that are true for you and edit them to say what you did. Undo puts everything back.

Job heading suggestions

For each role in the builder, suggests job titles the evidence supports: the official titles of the occupations your duties match, alternate titles whose wording already appears in your duties, and the posting's title when it fits the role. Only use a title that honestly describes what you did and your level, because employers check titles. Undo restores the old one.

Optional: local AI rewriting (experimental)

Rewrites your existing bullets so they use the posting's wording where it honestly describes the same work. The model runs in your browser on your device, and nothing is uploaded. It needs a desktop browser with WebGPU (recent Chrome or Edge) and a one-time download of about 1 GB, which the browser then keeps for later sessions. Every rewrite is checked automatically: it is dropped if it adds a number, or a capitalized name or tool, that is not already in your resume. You still review each one before it is applied.

Cover letter

Creates a draft from your Resume builder and the job posting on the Scan tab. It only uses facts from your resume, and it uses the template chosen in the builder. Read and edit it before sending.

Jobs and employers

Official government job sites and employer registers for the region you chose above. This page cannot search those sites for you, and it does not scrape them. The buttons open the official site in a new tab, with your job title and place filled in where the site supports it.

Search an employer register you downloaded

Download an official register from the links above, load it here, and search it on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Being on a register does not mean an employer has vacancies or will sponsor you, so always check the current job and the employer.

Library

Other applications built for you, in one place. Each opens in its own tab, and access to it is controlled by that application's own settings.

Release watch

This page cannot send push notifications or check the internet in the background. Instead, Release watch tells you each time you open the site which classifications are due for a check, and a banner appears on the Scan tab when one is. Expected dates come from the official sites when this page was built, so confirm them on the source page.

When a new release is out: open the official page, download the files, then either load them with the Load button in the Occupation data box on the Scan tab (this replaces the built-in data in your browser; an ESCO ZIP is remembered), or send the files to the Development Team to rebuild the built-in data. Then press "I checked" below. For real alerts, also subscribe to each agency's own email or release calendar.
